PitchPop Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

PitchPop is an ear-training app for kids made by Marika Lam. It's designed to collect as little as possible: no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. This policy covers the PitchPop iPhone app and the website at marikalam.github.io/apps/pitchpop.

Using PitchPop without an account

You don't need an account to play. Without one, everything PitchPop saves stays on your device: player names, which colors each player is learning, scores, and practice streaks. None of it is sent anywhere.

If you create an account

An account is optional and lets a family sync its players across devices. When you create one, we store:

  • your email address and password (the password is stored securely hashed, never readable),
  • each player's name and the colors they're learning.

Scores and streaks are not uploaded; they stay on each device. We use this information only to sign you in and keep your players in sync. We never sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

Accounts are stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, which also sends password-reset emails when you ask for one.

Children

PitchPop is made for kids to play, but accounts are meant to be created by a parent or guardian. A player profile only needs a first name or nickname. We don't knowingly collect other personal information from children, and kids don't need to enter any to play.

Speech and sounds

The voice that says color names, note names and scores is a set of short recordings that come with PitchPop. In the iPhone app they're built in; on the website they load from the same site as the rest of PitchPop. If a recording can't play, your device's own built-in voice says the words instead, on your device. No other services are used for speech.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account: in PitchPop, open your account (the button at the top right), then tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your email, password and synced players.
  • Delete data on a device: delete the PitchPop app, or clear the website's data in your browser settings.
  • You can also email us and we'll delete your account for you.
