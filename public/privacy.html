PitchPop Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

PitchPop is an ear-training app for kids made by Marika Lam. It's designed to collect as little as possible: no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking. This policy covers the PitchPop iPhone app and the website at marikalam.github.io/apps/pitchpop.

Your family account

PitchPop asks a parent or guardian to create a family account with an email address. It keeps each player's progress and stickers, and syncs the family's players across devices. For the account, we store:

  • your email address and password (the password is stored securely hashed, never readable),
  • each player's name and the colors they're learning,
  • each player's practice history (the date and length of each saved practice) and the days they finished a round of Pitch Practice, so practice time, tokens and streaks are the same on every device.

We use this information to sign you in and keep your players in sync, and the person who runs PitchPop can see the list of family account emails (when each joined and last signed in, and how many players it has). We never sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

Accounts are stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, which also sends password-reset emails when you ask for one.

Anonymous usage counts

To see how PitchPop is used, the app sends a few anonymous counts to our own server: that it was opened (and whether that's the first time today or the first time ever on that device), whether it's on an iPhone, iPad or the website, and when a round is finished, which activity it was, the language and the score. These counts have no name, email, account or device ID, so they can't be tied to you or your child. No third-party analytics service is used.

Children

PitchPop is made for kids to play, but accounts are meant to be created by a parent or guardian. A player profile only needs a first name or nickname. We don't knowingly collect other personal information from children, and kids don't need to enter any to play.

Speech and sounds

The voice that reads out color names is generated on your device. In the iPhone app, everything it needs is built in. On the website, the voice files are downloaded the first time from public file hosts (Hugging Face and jsDelivr), which, like any website, can see your IP address when your browser downloads from them. No text you type is sent to them.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account: in PitchPop, open your account (the button at the top right), then tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your email, password and synced players.
  • Delete data on a device: delete the PitchPop app, or clear the website's data in your browser settings.
  • You can also email us and we'll delete your account for you.
